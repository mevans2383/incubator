Your Roadmap

Here's how to use each tab

📋
My Profile
Build your recruiting profile
Fill out all 5 steps as honestly as possible. Your measurables, academics, high school program, career summary, and preferences all feed the algorithm. You can come back and edit anytime — re-submitting will refresh your school matches.
💡 Pro tip The career summary field is the most underrated input. The more detail you include about your stats, awards, and level of competition, the more accurate your matches will be.
🏟️
Schools
Explore your analytically matched schools
After submitting your profile, you'll see a division fit breakdown and 8 matched schools ranked by fit score. Each card shows why a school is a good fit and what to be aware of. Save any school to add it to your Action Plan.
💡 Pro tip Don't only save your top picks. Save a range — your best fits, a couple of reaches, and a safety or two. Your action plan will cover all of them.
📅
Action Plan ← Don't skip this
Follow your recruiting timeline
Every school you save generates a set of tasks — an intro email, a Twitter DM, a camp visit, and a follow-up — all organized into one chronological timeline. You'll also see seasonal social media tasks to keep your recruiting visible year-round.
🔥 This is the tab that matters most. Most athletes never reach out to coaches proactively. The ones who do get recruited. This tab gives you a step-by-step schedule so you never fall behind.
